Who I am

I am a civil engineer working on Public Works Department (PWD) road and drainage improvement projects in Manipur, on Package 03 (Imphal East Division) and Package 04 (Highway South Division). My work covers the full chain of quality on a road project: drain hydraulic and level calculations, method statements and Inspection and Test Plans, layer-by-layer site supervision, and clear technical replies to Non-Conformance Reports.
